# Logical Consistency — Toll

**Score: 2/5** — several major contradictions: Teresa's age and the eviction she lived through, what the scour drawdown does to the dam's head, and the gate being locked open twice. Add to that the reservoir depth, the season, and several recurring details that won't hold still, and the reader starts to distrust the story's facts.

## Findings

### 1. [major] The eviction Teresa remembers is not the one the reader saw
- **Where:** Scene 1 (and Scenes 8, 9)
- **Quote:** Scene 1: "Teresa did not put out her hand. … 'Keep your silver paper, Arthur. … you never bought the dirt under this sill'" / Scene 8: "'You were ten years old when the bailiffs cleared the orchards.' 'Eleven,' she said. … you signed the condemnation order with a gold pen on the hood of a county car while my mother was still loading the cookstove into the dray."
- **Problem:** In Scene 1 Teresa is plainly the adult householder, in an apron, refusing compensation on her own porch. No parents are there, no car, no bailiffs, and the family has no time to load anything: Myra drags her away as the water rises. Scene 8 recasts her as an eleven-year-old child with a mother present, and in Scene 9 the chimney becomes her father's. Scene 8 even gives her a "nursery". Teresa's age and her family are the story's own foundation.
- **Reader needed:** One version of the eviction: who Teresa was then, who else was there, and how it happened.

### 2. [major] The scour drawdown does opposite things to the dam's head
- **Where:** Scene 3 (and Scenes 6, 8)
- **Quote:** Scene 3: "laying the northern basin shelf bare under open air while leaving forty feet of water against the dam face" / Scene 8: "peel the shallow waters off the old Blackwood homestead without dropping the reservoir's operating head or starving the generators"
- **Problem:** Scene 3's own arithmetic takes the water at the wall from thirty fathoms (180 ft) down to forty feet, which is a huge drop in head. Scene 8 has the same calculation leave the head untouched, and Teresa's whole reconciliation depends on that. Myra's warning in Scene 6 is also about head pressure.
- **Reader needed:** The calculation should give the same result both times it is used.

### 3. [major] The scour gate is locked open in Scene 5, then locked open again in Scene 8
- **Where:** Scene 5 (and Scene 8)
- **Quote:** Scene 5: "the mechanical pawl engaged the inner ratchet, seating the lock dogs into the carriage of the low scour gate. … no switch at Arthur Finch's board could command the gate closed against the bronze wedge now holding the conduit open." / Scene 8: "the mechanical dog clicked home, locking the sediment scour valve into its open aperture"
- **Problem:** Scene 5 says the dogs are seated and the conduit is being held open, yet in the next breath the gate is only "primed". In Scene 8 Teresa does the same action again as if for the first time. The reader can't tell whether the drain began a night early.
- **Reader needed:** Scene 5 should leave the gate clearly primed but closed, so that Scene 8 is the first time it opens.

### 4. [minor] The reservoir's depth keeps changing
- **Where:** Scene 1 (and Scenes 2, 3, 5, 6, 8)
- **Quote:** Scene 3: "thirty fathoms deep against the masonry wall" / Scene 5: "the curved wall thickened against the ballast of eighty fathoms of captured basin"
- **Problem:** The water is thirty fathoms in Scenes 1, 2 and 3 and eighty fathoms in Scenes 5, 6 and 8. The gallery is also "three hundred feet below the crest", deeper than a thirty-fathom lake.
- **Reader needed:** One depth, used consistently.

### 5. [minor] The season changes overnight
- **Where:** Scene 6 (and Scenes 7, 9)
- **Quote:** Scene 6: "If you dump eighty fathoms into the riverbeds before summer" / Scene 9: "The hearth stones sat exposed beneath the naked sky, drying in the bitter autumn air"
- **Problem:** Myra's "before summer" places the story in spring. Scene 7's "every gallon it took in during the autumn freeze" suggests a thaw. Yet the next dawn is autumn.
- **Reader needed:** One season throughout.

### 6. [minor] The bronze key changes place and size
- **Where:** Scene 3 (and Scene 5)
- **Quote:** Scene 3: "Beside the inspection hatch hung an expanded-mesh locker … Within its cage, hooked to a rusted peg, hung the bronze manual spindle override key" / Scene 5: "At the terminus of the eastern passage … Inside, suspended between sets of heavy turnbuckles … It was a massive casting, nearly five feet in length"
- **Problem:** A key hanging from a peg in a mesh locker by the hatch becomes a five-foot casting slung in turnbuckles inside a padlocked cage at the far end of the passage.
- **Reader needed:** The same locker, in the same place, holding an object of the same size.

### 7. [minor] How long the telemetry has been out
- **Where:** Scene 7 (and Scene 8)
- **Quote:** Scene 7: "The telemetry out of High Ridge Gulch gave out twenty minutes ago" / Scene 8: "The telemetry is already blind, Arthur. Your instruments have been dead for two hours."
- **Problem:** Twenty minutes becomes two hours within the same night, and in Scene 7 Arthur is still reading working needles and a manometer.
- **Reader needed:** One timeline for the outage, or a clear sign that Teresa means Kester's jumpered circuits.

### 8. [nit] Recurring names and materials don't match
- **Where:** Scene 1 (and Scenes 2, 6, 8, 9)
- **Quote:** Scene 6: "Myra Vance—her cousin" vs Scene 2 "Mistress Vane" / Scene 1: "until only the brick chimney stood out" vs Scene 9 "the fieldstone chimney that Caleb Vane had laid"
- **Problem:** Vance and Vane read like one surname misspelled. The chimney is brick, then fieldstone. The authority is "provincial water commission" in Scene 1 and "Imperial Water Authority" in Scene 2. In Scene 8 the spindle bites "through twenty years of hardened tallow" that Kester cleaned and greased the night before.
- **Reader needed:** Make these match.

## What works
- Finch not recognising Teresa at the hire is set up and paid off in order: "He did not know her." (Scene 2) → "'Vane,' he whispered … 'Caleb Vane's girl.'" (Scene 8).
- The farm's depth on the shelf holds exactly: "submerged beneath only ten fathoms" (Scene 3) = "sixty feet of slate water" (Scene 6) = "ten fathoms of drowned quiet" (Scene 9).
- The cheater bar left in place carries cleanly from Scene 5 through Scene 6 to Scene 8: "She could leave the cheater bar where it lay."

## Out of lane
Arthur knows it is Myra's ward and that it is full of children ("Myra's ward is full of children") moments after first recognising Teresa. That is borderline knowledge, but it is plausible for the town's only clinic, so character or grounding may want to weigh in.
