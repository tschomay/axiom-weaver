# Setup and Payoff — The Borrowed Boat

**Score: 3/5** — the cracked stay, the seven-day waiver and Helen's sale are planted and paid off well, but the central reveal (Arthur's deathbed instruction about Clara) is a cheat, because earlier scenes show Ruth not knowing it.

## Findings

### 1. [major] The deathbed promise overturns what Ruth was shown not knowing
- **Where:** Scene 5 (against Scene 1 and Scene 2)
- **Quote:** "three lines in her father's tidy copperplate caught her eye … Arthur had never owned land in Tern Bay, not on any deed Ruth had ever seen" (Scene 1); "He gave me these coordinates and made me promise to carry him to this door. A woman named Clara." (Scene 5)
- **Problem:** Scene 1 shows Ruth finding the coordinates for the first time and puzzling over them. Scene 2 has her thinking "whatever Arthur had hidden at Tern Bay would stay drowned in his arithmetic." Then Scene 5 says he gave her the coordinates in person, named Clara and made her promise. The reveal depends on knowledge the story showed her not having, and the reader was never shown the promise, Clara, or a second household.
- **Reader needed:** Either Ruth's close-third scenes should carry some sign that she already knows (a promise she is dreading, a name she won't say), or the Clara reveal should come from the evidence she actually finds.

### 2. [minor] Ruth's lie about the stay is never found out
- **Where:** Scene 2 (and Scene 3, Scene 4)
- **Quote:** "\"Sound enough,\" she called back, her voice level." (Scene 2); "You never could be bothered to tie a thing down. Not the painter. Not Donald's seven days. Not him." (Scene 3)
- **Problem:** Ruth hides a known fracture, and then attacks Helen for carelessness. The stay breaks and nearly sinks them (Scene 4). This hypocrisy is set up clearly, but no one ever acknowledges it, so an obvious reckoning between the sisters never happens.
- **Reader needed:** One beat where Helen realises, or Ruth admits, that Ruth knew about the stay, or at least where Ruth visibly feels it.

### 3. [minor] Helen knows about the coordinates without being shown learning them
- **Where:** Scene 5
- **Quote:** "\"The logbook coordinates,\" Helen called back over the diesel's clatter. \"They were never an offshore fueling stop.\""
- **Problem:** Only Ruth is shown reading the margin (Scene 1). Helen's sudden knowledge, and Ruth's flat "I know," turn a discovery into something already agreed offstage.
- **Reader needed:** A moment where Helen sees the log or Ruth shows her, so the two sisters' shared realisation is earned.

### 4. [minor] The lost tender is given a consequence that never comes
- **Where:** Scene 3
- **Quote:** "And now we have no dinghy, no tender, and nowhere to go ashore if the hull opens up under us."
- **Problem:** The loss gets a full, vivid scene and an explicit stake. Then the dismasting (Scene 4) and the landing (Scene 5) happen as if the tender never mattered. It ends up working only as a trigger for the argument.
- **Reader needed:** Either the missing tender should limit something later (the landing, the escape from the squall), or its stake should not be stated so loudly.

### 5. [minor] Arthur's debts drive the sale and then disappear
- **Where:** Scene 5 (and Scene 6)
- **Quote:** "pay Arthur's timber accounts, and settle the shop debts before the bailiff moved in." / "We were drowning in his arrears, Ruth." (Scene 5)
- **Problem:** The debts are Helen's whole reason for the breaker deal. Scene 6 takes on new drydock costs and returns the deposit, but never says how the arrears or the bailiff will be dealt with.
- **Reader needed:** One line that answers or deliberately sets aside the debt.

### 6. [nit] Clara is named and then left in doubt
- **Where:** Scene 5
- **Quote:** "\"And leave her to weep over what he left behind.\"" / "the untended lawn lay drowned under nettles and sea-plantain"
- **Problem:** The overgrown, blind-windowed cottage hints that Clara may be gone, while the sisters talk as if she is inside. The story doesn't signal whether that ambiguity is intended.
- **Reader needed:** A small sign of whether anyone lives there, so the sisters' refusal to touch the porch lands as defiance and not guesswork.

## What works
- The cracked tang is a model plant and payoff: "a hairline fracture split the bronze like a seam in cooling hearth-slag" (Scene 2) → "The bronze tang sheared clean through its scarred grain." (Scene 4).
- The seven-day waiver is a clean clock, checked in along the way ("six days left", "three days left") and resolved in Scene 6: "Three hours remain on the yellow slipway notice pinned to the harbour office wall."
- Helen's sale is planted fairly before it is confessed: "calculating timber footage and what a buyer down south might pay for thirty feet of seasoned larch" (Scene 1) → "I took a deposit from a breaker at the commercial docks" (Scene 5).

## Out of lane
The urn is a "brass cylinder", a "jar" and a "sealed tin urn", and Helen's absence is "five winters" in Inverness, then "three years" in the south (consistency).
