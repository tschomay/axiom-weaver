# Setup and Payoff — Toll

**Score: 3/5** — The vow, the chisel, the aquifer warning and the eviction warrant all pay off strongly, but the story's central reversal (the karst cavern that only the water's weight holds up) is dropped: Teresa drains the lake anyway and the promised collapse never comes or is explained away.

## Findings

### 1. [major] The karst cavern, the story's biggest stake, never pays off
- **Where:** Scene 9, Scene 11, Scene 12 (never resolved in Scene 13)
- **Quote:** Scene 9: "To vent the scour gate—to fulfill her vow and drain the water back down to her father's hearth datum—meant unseating that ballast. It meant tearing the plug from a hollow mountain." Scene 12: "And the northern caverns will answer for it."
- **Problem:** Scene 9 turns the story on this fact: draining the reservoir brings down the ridge and "liquidated every settlement down to the delta within forty minutes." Teresa accepts it and lets go of the chisel. In Scene 11 she opens the scour gate anyway, and Scene 12 raises the threat again. But the lake drains to the hearth and nothing happens to the ridge. The only consequence in Scene 13 is dry wells. Either Kroll's revelation was false or the story forgot it, and the story never says which.
- **Reader needed:** a payoff for the cavern: it collapses, or it holds, or someone realises Kroll had exaggerated. As written, the reader is left waiting for a catastrophe that never comes.

### 2. [major] Teresa's decision to open the gate goes against what she has just learned
- **Where:** Scene 11 (against Scene 9)
- **Quote:** Scene 11: "'The town will endure silt,' Teresa said, the decision falling like a portcullis. 'They will not drown in a tidal wave. But the river will have its datum back.'"
- **Problem:** Two scenes earlier the scour gate itself was shown to be the tidal-wave risk ("tearing the plug from a hollow mountain"). Her line treats Pike's dynamite as the only flood danger and the gate as mere silt, so the climax rests on a distinction the story never set up. The narrator covers the gap with "the vow of the dry hearth pulled at her like an open wound" rather than letting the reader follow her thinking.
- **Reader needed:** a reason visible on the page for Teresa to think a controlled drain avoids the collapse (a detail in the bore logs, a reading from Kroll's chart), or a clear sign that she knows the risk and takes it anyway.

### 3. [minor] The karst revelation is retold two more times
- **Where:** Scene 10 and Scene 11 (after Scene 9)
- **Quote:** Scene 11: "his cracked voice cutting through the rising squall to lay bare the truth he had hidden for years: the deep gypsum cavern yawning in the northern canyon wall, an unstable karst pocket that would have swallowed the cliffs"
- **Problem:** Scene 10 has Teresa retell it to Nomi, which does dramatic work. Scene 11 then opens with a narrator's recap of the same reveal, as if the story were reminding itself. The repetition weakens a reversal that already lands once.
- **Reader needed:** Scene 11 to start from Teresa's unsettled state, not a summary of Scene 9.

### 4. [minor] The carved 740 is replaced by a different mark
- **Where:** Scene 2 and Scene 12
- **Quote:** Scene 2: "driving the steel into the native rock until four numerals were bitten into the shoulder of the gorge: 740." Scene 12: "The white painted benchmark on the northern rock rib stood dry and peeling in the dawn breeze."
- **Problem:** The mark Teresa cuts with her father's chisel is one of the story's most charged setups, and the payoff turns to a painted benchmark and an "iron spike on their survey deed" instead. The carving she made is never seen again.
- **Reader needed:** the falling water to uncover or pass her own carved numerals.

### 5. [minor] The two-stage interlock and the bypass line are set up in detail, then skipped
- **Where:** Scene 4 and Scene 5, against Scene 11
- **Quote:** Scene 4: "That emergency gate will not budge an inch unless the secondary pressure release cylinder is engaged first with the master brass interlock key." Scene 11: "She lifted the two-foot cruciform key and drove its brass teeth squarely into the emergency interlock"
- **Problem:** Teresa studies the bypass line, the equalization circuit and the sump pressurisation, and Pike tells her she is "tracing the wrong loop". At the climax she needs none of it. The key alone opens the gate. The shearing Callow warned of does pay off in Scene 12 (the gate "won't dog back"), but the procedure the story set up as the obstacle is dropped.
- **Reader needed:** a climax that uses, or visibly skips, the step she prepared in Scene 4.

### 6. [minor] Kroll knows who she is and never deals with the stolen key
- **Where:** Scene 9 (and Scene 8)
- **Quote:** Scene 9: "I knew the name the morning you handed your papers to Callow. Teresa Vance."
- **Problem:** The reveal fairly explains why he let her pass in Scene 8. But having recognised her and warned her what the gate would do, the engineer never asks for the key or alerts anyone, and he then turns up by chance in Scene 11. The story raises the question of what he meant to do about her and never answers it.
- **Reader needed:** a line showing Kroll's choice about the key (he leaves it with her on purpose, or he means to report it).

### 7. [minor] Pike's sabotage motive comes out of nowhere
- **Where:** Scene 11 (against Scene 5)
- **Quote:** Scene 11: "'They took my river,' Julian snarled ... 'They took everything we had and called it municipal equity.'"
- **Problem:** Scene 5 plants his contempt for Kroll ("Let him explain an empty flume"), so a betrayal is fair. But his own loss, and the dynamite, are first mentioned at the climax, which makes his turn feel convenient rather than earned.
- **Reader needed:** one earlier hint that Pike has his own drowned claim, or that he is up to something beyond Teresa's plan.

### 8. [nit] The ditch-side meeting in Scene 3 leads nowhere
- **Where:** Scene 3
- **Quote:** "'The culvert's choked, Kroll,' she said, her voice dropping into the ditch like a dropped stone."
- **Problem:** The scene builds up to a confrontation, then cuts off, and nothing in the next scenes refers back to it.
- **Reader needed:** some later callback to this first meeting.

## What works
- Nomi's aquifer warning is set up twice and pays off in full: Scene 6's "You will choke every well in this valley to dry dust before winter is out" is realised by Scene 13's "Every bore in the lower terrace ran dry within an hour of your gate opening."
- The eviction warrant left "pinned to the bench under the cold weight of the valley fog" in Scene 1 turns up again in the silt in Scene 13 ("its ink washed into faint indigo shadows beneath the muck"), a quiet and earned callback.
- The chisel carries weight in every act (the 740 carving, prying the locker, cutting Pike's firing leads), and the kept vow lands with "It felt like nothing at all."

## Out of lane
The time the reservoir stood full is "Ten years" in Scene 3 but "three summers" and "three years" in Scene 12 (consistency).
